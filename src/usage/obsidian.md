1. Copy a flavour's folder from this folder into your vault's `.obsidian/themes/`, keeping the folder name.
2. Pick it under Settings > Appearance > Themes.

Needs Obsidian 1.0 or newer, the `minAppVersion` in each `manifest.json`. Each flavour is its
own theme and paints both of Obsidian's colour schemes, so the Appearance light/dark switch
leaves the flavour alone.
