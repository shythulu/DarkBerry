The palette

Fourteen accents, twelve neutrals, a jam for buttons and a tint for the active tab. Every colour in hex, RGB, HSL and OKLCH. Click a swatch to copy its hex, or any value to copy that form.

The same roles drive every app: keywords are berry everywhere, errors are cranberry everywhere, and the active tab always carries the tint.

Where it comes from

Dark PurpleBenjamin Moore 2073-10
#4b3540
  • One paint colour at the root. Dark Purple is Wisp's text, Fen's background and the raised surfaces in Mire, and every neutral in Darkberry is built around its plum.
  • Modelled on Catppuccin. Four flavours, twelve neutrals with Catppuccin's names and jobs, the same palette file format, and the same terminal colour rules.
  • Checked on every build. Text you read stays at 4.5:1 contrast or better, and code colours stay far enough apart that keywords, functions and types never blur together.
  • Four berry tints. Lingonberry, cloudberry, crowberry and blueberry each lean Darkberry's backgrounds toward a berry, spaced evenly around the colour wheel.
